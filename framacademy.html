FramAcademy is another Masters project where we had fun imagining an offer that would meet students' needs and desires within Fram, a French agency company. From defining the target audience to building a strategy and shaping the visual identity, we thought about what we'd love to see exist in real life, and worked to make it as believable and concrete as possible, at least within a school setting. It was an inspiring exercise that let us explore every step of the creative process, while developing a concept that's fun, useful, and full of potential.

Big thanks again to Caroline, Faratina and Louise! <3
