2018 · First-Year Project · Packaging Kolors is my final project for my first year of Licence in Graphic Design & Print Media. I designed everything, from the concept to the packaging and print campaigns, in 2018. Kolors is a beer flavored with fruits and herbs, distributed by Kronenbourg with the aim of revamping its image among the general public, especially young adults.

Looking back, the branding is perhaps a little too acid-pop and could attract the wrong target. However, I really love this project graphically, it was my first real graphic design project where I had Carte blanche on every detail, from client to brand direction, and I just started to learn digital creative tools, so I did a lot of experimenting and manual work to provide real life mock ups of the bottles.
Kult was a media outlet specializing in underground and urban culture. We organized cultural and musical events in iconic venues such as the MAMAC and Le 109. With Jump Arounds, showcases and block parties, Kult got the youth of Nice moving for a solid three years, in a city where young people and urban initiatives can sometimes be stifled. I proudly represented my role as the association's Graphic Designer from 2019 to 2021!

Editorial & Publishing Migration is a design and publishing project I did during my second year of Licence in Graphic Design & Print Media. It has three axis with three intentions. The yellow one plays with geometry, where everything fits like a puzzle you assemble as you read. The red one highlights the daily ordeal of migrants, through a cryptic layout that requires effort from the reader. The blue one highlights the human aspect, with impactful double-page images linked to crucial information.
Muséum d'Histoire Naturelle de Nice · Campaign The following posters were created during my second year of Licence in Graphic Design & Print Media. The Natural History Museum of Nice has a reputation for being a somewhat outdated museum. For me, it was the dusty museum I had visited in elementary school. No kid in school wanted to go there, we were spooked about the old furniture, dusty frames and stuffed animals. The brief was to create an offbeat campaign to boost communication and attract young visitors to this museum steeped in history, and shake off this outdated reputation by being fun and slighty offbeat compared to usual museum campaigns. There are a few axis I went for, experimenting with drawing, handmade typography and a lot of psd.

This fictional commercial was made in 2021 as part of my Masters' degree' advertising workshop.

The brief: Imagine, storyboard, film, and edit a fictional commercial for Deezer.

I made it with my friends Louise, Caroline, and Faratina, as per usual! We started with a simple observation: despite our very different musical (and fashion) styles, we got along really well right away, and we were inseparable at the first time we teamed up.

The idea behind the commercial was to depict a group of friends in their everyday lives. Each one listens to her favorite music until one of them shares a song with the others via a collaborative playlist. The friends receive a notification, play the music on their way to meet up, and end up at one of their homes for a collaborative brunch, their weekend get-together.

This commercial highlights the shared playlist feature on Deezer, and above all, the connection it can create between people.

Many thanks to the girls for this project and these beautiful digital memories of our Master's years. <3
